About Us

Welcome to Woodruff Bicycle Shop, your hometown hub for all things cycling! Located right on Main Street in the heart of Woodruff, SC, we’re proud to serve our community with a passion for bikes and a love for the open road. Whether you're a seasoned rider, weekend adventurer, or just getting started, our friendly team is here to help you find the perfect bike, gear, and service to match your lifestyle.


We specialize in new bike sales, repairs, tune-ups, accessories, and expert advice to keep you riding smooth and safe. Our shop carries trusted brands and offers something for every rider—road bikes, mountain bikes, hybrids, and e-bikes too.


At Woodruff Bicycle Shop, it's more than just a business—it's a community. Stop by, talk shop, grab a coffee, and join one of our local group rides. We’re here to keep Woodruff rolling, one pedal at a time.
